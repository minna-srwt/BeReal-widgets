📏 The rubric

Specific · The test question

Could someone picture exactly who struggles, when, and what you'd measure?

0 · Not yet"Users" and "engagement", with no moment or metric
1 · Getting thereNames the user or the moment, but not both, or the impact has no measure
2 · StrongNames the user type, the exact moment, the outcome, and one thing to measure
Connected · The test question

Can a reader trace every piece of evidence back to your teardown or organizer?

0 · Not yetNo evidence, or "people complain"
1 · Getting thereSome evidence is traceable, or the Box 3 label is missing or upgraded
2 · Strong2–3 pieces, each pointing to a moment # or a Box and row, with the Box 3 label carried over honestly
Committed · The test question

Do you make one clear call, with confidence that matches your evidence?

0 · Not yetSeveral problems, or a list of features, or "High" with nothing behind it
1 · Getting thereOne problem, but a vague opportunity or no reason for the confidence
2 · StrongOne problem, one opportunity, and a confidence level with a reason
