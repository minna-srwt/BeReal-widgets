Lookout is a security app that protects your phone. Right after leaving Dropbox, Sean ran this question for Lookout, and only 7% of users said they'd be "very disappointed" without it.

Two weeks later, that number was 40%.

How did they move it that fast? Hold that thought. You'll find out when you analyze your own answers in Step 5.

Two honest limits before you use it:

  • Sean normally asks only people who have really used a product, "not people who've seen a demo." Your participants will have clicked a wireframe for a few minutes.
  • He likes about 30 responses before trusting the number. You'll have 5–10.

So treat your result as an early signal: worth reading closely, not worth announcing.

✅ Pro Tip: use short, neutral phrasing. Each question should take under 10 seconds to read.

45678📋Format your response scales

For your 1–5 questions, use consistent labels so results are comparable later. Example scale:

1️⃣ = Very Difficult / Not Authentic / Not Likely

2️⃣ = Difficult / Slightly Inauthentic / Unlikely

3️⃣ = Neutral

4️⃣ = Easy / Feels Authentic / Likely

5️⃣ = Very Easy / Feels Real / Very Likely

Why: consistency means everyone reads every scale the same way, which matters when you compare answers later.

Write your labels in Scale Labels first.

✅You're on track if every number on every scale has a word label.
