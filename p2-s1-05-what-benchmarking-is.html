Let's begin with this short video on what competitive benchmarking really means and why product teams rely on it.

Key takeaway: Competitive benchmarking is how product managers measure their product's features, performance, or user experience against others in the same space.

You'll often hear it mixed up with competitive analysis. Both help you understand the competition, but they zoom to different levels:

Competitive analysis
Competitive benchmarking
Looks broadly at how competitors operate: market position, audience, strategy
Goes deep on specific features or metrics, side by side
Answers "Who are we up against, and how do they play?"
Answers "Who does this better, and why?"

Competitive benchmarking

Studying the specific moves that win it

Why Product Managers Use Benchmarking

Benchmarking helps PMs make sense of a crowded market. It shows what users already expect, and where your product can stand out.

A product manager uses it to answer questions like:

?How do our features stack up against top competitors?
?Where are we falling short, or over-investing?
?Which competitor patterns reveal user needs we haven't noticed?
