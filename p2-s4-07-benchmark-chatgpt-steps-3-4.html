If everyone outsources their thinking to AI, everyone ends up thinking about the same thing.

That's not how you learn to think, or how you build a point of view of your own. The AI can line the themes up. You decide what the numbers mean.

3

Compare Category Scores and Spot Patterns

You already have Theme Scores for both apps. Now interpret what they mean side by side. You're looking for where users feel strongest, and how those feelings differ. Ask yourself:

Which themes have the highest overall scores?
Are those themes positive reasons to come back (drivers) or negative friction (barriers)?
Are there themes where both apps score high, but for different reasons?
Which feelings seem to keep users returning, and which might make them drop off?
4

Draw Insights

Finally, turn your findings into 3–5 key insights and a clear recommendation for BeReal: who wins, why, and what BeReal could learn. Ask yourself:

Which app's feature creates stronger daily engagement, and why?
Which feature produces healthier, more sustainable feelings?
What could BeReal adjust to stay authentic but bring people back more consistently?

Then put every insight through April Dunford's two tests:

Better how?
For whom?

An insight that says one app is "better" without saying better how, and for whom, isn't ready yet.

Now you have everything you need to build your comparison table.
