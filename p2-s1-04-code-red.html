In December 2022, a shock hit Mountain View, Google's hometown.

ChatGPT exploded onto the scene, and inside Google the mood turned urgent enough to earn a label: "code red."

Leadership re-routed teams to respond. The risk wasn't just losing buzz. It was losing the very habit of "Googling" when people wanted answers.

Let's watch BBC tech correspondent James Clayton break down how ChatGPT's viral success sent Google racing to respond.

Teams were told to study the rival like scientists dissecting a specimen. What made ChatGPT so powerful?

Direct answers. Conversational flow. A sense that it did the thinking for you.

Those observations became Google's new blueprint. Within months, Google launched Bard and began weaving AI summaries directly into Search through the Search Generative Experience (SGE), transforming the familiar white search box into something new.

That's competitive benchmarking in motion: watch a rival set a new bar, study why it works, then build your own version.
