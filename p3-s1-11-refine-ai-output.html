Highlight promising ideas.

Which ones instantly feel right for BeReal's tone and mission?

2

Apply the quick filter:

❌Violates authenticity or privacy? → drop it
⚙️Needs heavy infrastructure or moderation? → drop it
💬Feels light, genuine, and habit-forming? → keep it
3

Re-prompt for better versions.

For example:

"Take ideas #2 and #5 and make them simpler. Imagine they could be prototyped as a simple clickable wireframe."
"Rewrite these ideas to make them feel more spontaneous and less gamified."
4

Find different paths to the same idea.

Remember Latchet's founders? They had one problem and four very different ways to solve it. John Zeratsky compares it to Google Maps: same destination, but one route is faster, another avoids tolls.

Ask AI to do the same with your favorite idea:

4

Find different paths to the same idea.

"Take idea #3. Give me 3 very different ways BeReal could deliver the same benefit to users. For example, one as a notification, one as a new tab, one as a change to how posting works."

Two ideas solving the same problem in different ways makes your next step, choosing between them, much more interesting.
