Why You're Learning This:

BeReal's goal is simple but ambitious — increase Monthly Active Users.

To get there, product teams need to understand both what the designed journey looks like, and how real users actually behave inside it.

designed real users

Mapping the journey is step one: it gives you a baseline of the intended experience, so that later you can compare it to what actually happens.

The gap between the two is often exactly why users churn — or why they come back.
