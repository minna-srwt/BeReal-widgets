What Good Looks Like

This task is checked against three criteria from the rubric:

📌 These examples use Duolingo, the app from the training walkthrough, so you can see what the rubric looks for. The person filling them in did a teardown of Duolingo first. Your organizer will be about BeReal.

📏 The rubric

Specific · The test question

Could someone find the exact number or quote you mean?

0 · Not yetVague claims, no figures or dates
1 · Getting thereSome numbers have an exact figure and a date, but others are rounded, undated, or quotes are paraphrased
2 · StrongEvery number has its exact figure, its date, and whether it's reported or estimated. Every quote is copied word for word.
Connected · The test question

Can a reader trace every number, quote, and ✓ back to its source?

0 · Not yetNo links, or ✓ marks with nothing behind them
1 · Getting thereMost items are linked, but some ✓ marks or numbers can't be traced, or no second source was checked
2 · StrongEvery number and quote links to its source, every number had a second source checked, and every ✓ points to a real row or teardown moment #
Committed · The test question

Do you make a clear call about what the data says, and how sure you are?

0 · Not yetLists data without a verdict, or calls everything a "pattern"
1 · Getting thereGives a verdict, but ignores the pattern rule or a conflict, or changes confidence without saying why
2 · StrongLabels each theme 🟢 / 🟡 / 🔴 using the 3-source rule, picks backs up / complicates / contradicts with a reason, and updates confidence honestly
