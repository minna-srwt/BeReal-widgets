Step 1 of 6
Part C

Build Your Journey Map

Read your whole moment log from top to bottom, across every session. Then group your moments into stages, and name each stage yourself.

A new stage usually starts when:

your goal changes (for example, from "get in" to "post something")
the app asks something new of you
you leave the app and come back
you're waiting for something

Step 1 of 6
Part C

Build Your Journey Map

For each stage, fill in the row: its moment #s, what you did, thought and felt, whether it was a high or a low, and why it's its own stage.

🤖AI allowed: paste in your moment log and ask AI to suggest groupings. Then decide for yourself. The stage names and the reasons have to be yours.

Part D, 1–2

Name the Core Mechanic and What's Working

1Core mechanic. In one sentence: what's the single design decision that defines how BeReal works?
2What's working. Pick one specific moment from your log, give its #, and explain why it works: what does it make you feel or do?

Step 3 of 6
Part D, 3

Find What's Broken

Gia's team has a name for this move: they "zoom in on… where is the experience most broken right now."

Pick one low point from your map, give its moment #, and describe the moment, not just the feeling.

Vague"Posting feels stressful."
Specific"The notification fires while I'm in class, and there's no way to post later without a 'late' label."

Step 4 of 6
Part D, 4

Ask Why BeReal Built It That Way

Take the design choice behind your broken moment, and ask the teardown question: what did the team choose, and what did it give up to get it?

1Write 2–3 possible reasons the team chose it.
2Then name the trade-off: what does BeReal gain, and what does it give up?

Step 4 of 6
Part D, 4

🤖 This is a good place for the thinking-partner prompt from the training:

I think BeReal designed [a feature you picked] because [your reason]. Give me two other explanations I haven't considered, and tell me what evidence would show that my explanation is wrong.

Keep only the explanations you can defend.
