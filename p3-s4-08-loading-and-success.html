What it is: the short time when something is happening behind the scenes: data is being fetched, a photo is uploading, the feature is starting up.

Why it matters: even a 1-second delay feels long with no feedback. A loading state reassures users that something is happening.

Example: when BeReal is fetching your camera or your partner's photo:

"Finding your Duo…"(Add a small progress icon or spinner.)

Design tip: don't overdo it. Subtle movement or text is enough. Never say just "Loading…": make it specific to the moment.

Emotion to aim for: anticipation, not frustration.
3️⃣

Success State – The Feel-Good Moment

What it is: confirmation that something worked: your post went live, your pairing was accepted, your reminder was sent.

Why it matters: this is your moment of delight. It tells users they did something, and it encourages them to do it again. It's also your chance to give them a reason to come back.

Example: after posting a joint photo:

"You and Maya posted your BeReal! 🔆""See what your friends shared today."

Design tip:

  • Keep visuals light but celebratory.
  • Use BeReal's casual tone ("Nice!" instead of "Submission successful").
  • A tiny highlight color or confetti cue is plenty. No overload.
Emotion to aim for: satisfaction, connection, quiet joy.
