Keep your friends close and your enemies closer.

Michael Corleone's advice from The Godfather Part II has outlived the mafia world that inspired it.

Keep your friends close and your enemies competitors closer.

Today, it's practically a product strategy rule, except the "enemies" are your competitors, and keeping them close means understanding them deeply.

In business, competitors aren't villains. They're chasing the same customers, attention, and market share you are.
