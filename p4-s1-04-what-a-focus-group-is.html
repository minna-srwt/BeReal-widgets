What is a Focus Group?

A focus group is a small, structured discussion (usually 5–10 people) led by a facilitator who asks open-ended questions to understand people's experiences, opinions, and reactions.

In this project, your "product" is your BeReal feature prototype, and your focus group is your test audience (friends, classmates, or peers).

🎤

Your job will be to:

⚖️Introduce your idea neutrally (without nudging people toward an answer)
👆Let participants explore the prototype
❓Guide the discussion with thoughtful, open questions
👀Notice tone, expressions, and reactions
✍️Record what people say and how they say it
Why product managers use focus groups

Product teams use focus groups when they need depth, not data points.
