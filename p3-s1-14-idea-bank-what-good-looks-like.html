What Good Looks Like

📌 These examples design a new feature for Spotify (rival: Apple Music), carrying on from Project 2. The research, quotes and ideas are made up for illustration. Notice what the strong one has: its own research first, then the AI, then hard filtering.

📏 The rubric

Part A: Research you can point to · The test question

Could someone else find each input in your Project 1 and 2 work?

0 · Not yetBlank, or general ("users find it boring")
1 · Getting thereFilled in, but one input is vague: no named feature, no status-quo sentence, or friction themes without quotes
2 · StrongNamed competitor features, your status-quo sentence, and 2–3 friction themes, each with a quote
Part B: Wide, then filtered · The test question

Did you go wide with the AI, then do the filtering yourself?

0 · Not yetOnly a few ideas, or no prompt, or no filter verdicts
1 · Getting thereA full list, but fewer than half trace to Part A, or every idea is kept
2 · Strong~10 ideas from a prompt that used your research. At least half point to Part A, every idea has a filter verdict, and at least one is dropped with a reason
Part C: Ideas only BeReal could ship · The test question

Are your 5–7 ideas testable, distinct, and giving you a real choice?

0 · Not yetFewer than 5, hypotheses with no reason, or ideas any app could have
1 · Getting there5–7 ideas with reasons, but one could fit any app, or no two solve the same problem
2 · Strong5–7 ideas. Every hypothesis says because…, every idea passes Authentic / Focused / Distinct, and two solve the same problem differently

Feature Idea Table
1 · "Friday Fresh"Users will open the app on Fridays more often because the mix they'd started to tune out has something new.
2 · "Mix Swap"Users will open the app more often because they're curious what their friend's day sounds like.
Feature Idea Table
3 · "Skip Learns"Users will quit fewer sessions early because the mix responds before they give up.
4 · "Monthly Mini-Wrapped"Users will open the app at least monthly because there's a fresh look at their own listening.
Feature Idea Table
5 · "Drive Mix"Users will open the app on drives because it's faster than finding a station.
Same problem, different pathsIdeas #1 and #2 both fix the stale mix, one by adding new songs and one by borrowing a friend's taste.
How it scores
Part A: 2 Named features, a specific status quo (YouTube in the background, the car radio), and each friction theme has a quote.
Part B: 2 The prompt pastes the research in. Most ideas point to Part A, every idea has a verdict, and two are dropped with reasons.
Part C: 2 Every hypothesis says because, each idea uses something only Spotify has, and #1 and #2 give a real choice.
