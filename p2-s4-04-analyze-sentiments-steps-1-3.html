1

Understand What We Mean by "User Sentiment"

User sentiment is how people feel about a specific feature. For example:

"I love that BeReal feels spontaneous." → Positive: authenticity
"I always miss the 2-minute window." → Negative: time pressure

Patterns like these explain why people come back every day, or why they stop.

2

The AI Workflow You'll Use

You'll use two tools together:

1 · Perplexity
to find real, recent user quotes
2 · ChatGPT (or Claude)
to organize those quotes into themes

If everyone outsources their thinking to AI, everyone ends up thinking about the same thing.

That's not how you learn to think, or how you build a point of view of your own. The AI finds and organizes. You check and decide.

3

Use Perplexity to Gather Real Quotes

Perplexity searches real sources such as Reddit, App Store reviews, and TikTok comments, and links to where it found each one. Example prompt:

"Find as many recent user reviews or Reddit posts as you can (ideally 50–100) discussing BeReal's / [Selected Competitor]'s [Chosen Product Feature]. Include both positive and negative opinions, one quote per line, with a link to the source for each."
3

If You Get Too Few

If you get too few, refine:

"Can you include more examples from Reddit, TikTok, and the App Store?"

Pro Tip: Make sure every quote mentions the specific feature, not just the app in general.
