A few years ago, a startup called SparkToro launched with everything going for it.

SparkToro is an audience research tool that helps marketers figure out who their customers really are and where to find them — built by Rand Fishkin, best known for co-founding Moz, one of the biggest names in SEO. People paid attention.

By every early signal, the product looked like it was working.

Except it wasn't.

People who signed up for the free version weren't sticking around to pay for it — and nobody on the team could say exactly why.

They didn't fix it with more ads, a slicker signup flow, or a redesign.
