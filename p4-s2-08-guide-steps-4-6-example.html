🧾 BeReal Feature Focus Group — Discussion Guide

Feature: Daily Duo · Duration: 35 minutes · Facilitator: [Your Name] · Date: [Insert date]

Warm-Up (5 mins)

  • How often do you use BeReal?
  • What do you enjoy most about it?

Interest (5–7 mins)

  • What's your first impression when you see this feature?
  • Would you want to try it if it launched on BeReal? Follow-up: Why or why not?

Usability (10–12 mins)

  • When you clicked through the prototype, was anything confusing or unclear?
  • What do you think happens on each screen? Follow-up: What gave you that impression?

Value & Impact (10–12 mins)

  • Would this feature make you open BeReal more often? Follow-up: Why does that matter to you?
  • How does this feature make BeReal feel: more real, less real, or about the same?
  • If this feature didn't exist, what would you do instead?

Wrap-Up (3–5 mins)

  • If you could change one thing about this feature, what would it be?
  • Would you want BeReal to actually launch it?
