What Good Looks Like

📌 These examples compare Spotify and Apple Music, so you can see what a strong Feature Map looks like. Yours will be about BeReal and your rival. Notice what the strong one has: the person's own thinking at every Part.

📏 The rubric

Part A: Your own read · The test question

Is each feature something users do or receive, and does it say what brings them back?

0 · Not yetQualities or feelings ("it's authentic", "users like it")
1 · Getting thereMostly real features, but some explanations stay general
2 · StrongEvery feature is a real action or prompt, with a specific behavior. At least one competitor feature has no BeReal match
Part B: AI as a starting point · The test question

Did you test the AI instead of trusting it?

0 · Not yetPasted the answers, no check, no take
1 · Getting thereSome testing: a source opened, or a push-back, or a take, but not all
2 · StrongA source opened with a verdict, a push-back with any swap explained, and your own take on what the AI caught or got wrong
Part C: Your final call · The test question

Does each final feature earn its place, and does the competitor line up with the final BeReal table?

0 · Not yetRepeats, or features kept because "the AI said so"
1 · Getting there3–6 unique features, but a "Why it's in" or "Better how?" is thin ("better UI")
2 · Strong3–6 unique features, each with a reason it stays. "Better how?" names what a user would feel
