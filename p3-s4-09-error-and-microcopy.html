What it is: what users see when something fails: a network issue, a photo upload crash, a pairing that doesn't go through.

🖼️"Hmm, looks like your Duo didn't load. Want to retry?"
RetryTry again later

Why it matters: errors are inevitable, and good design expects them. A kind error message keeps users from leaving the app frustrated.

Example:

"Hmm, looks like your Duo didn't load. Want to retry?"(Two buttons: "Retry" and "Try again later.")

Design tip:

  • Use clear, conversational language (no technical jargon).
  • Offer at least one way to recover.
  • Keep the tone neutral. Never blame the user.
Emotion to aim for: reassurance, calm recovery.

The Power of Microcopy

Microcopy = the short bits of text that make your feature feel human. At BeReal, the tone should always feel like a friend, not a system.

Robotic Microcopy ❌"No data available."
BeReal-Style Microcopy ✅"Nothing here yet. Start your first post!"
Robotic Microcopy ❌"Loading…"
BeReal-Style Microcopy ✅"Hang tight, getting your Duo ready."
Robotic Microcopy ❌"Action completed."
BeReal-Style Microcopy ✅"Nice! You posted with your Duo."
Robotic Microcopy ❌"Error code 400."
BeReal-Style Microcopy ✅"That didn't go through. Let's try again."

These small details build trust and warmth.
