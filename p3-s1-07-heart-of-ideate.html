Think with empathy.

Each idea should connect back to a real user insight from your earlier projects.

Example: if users said they forget to post, design something that reminds them gently, not something that pressures them.

Design around BeReal's essence.

Every idea must fit BeReal's identity:

Real,not performative
Spontaneous,not addictive
Light,not polished
