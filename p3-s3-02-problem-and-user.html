Target User

Who exactly are you designing this for? Narrow it down: not "everyone on BeReal," but a specific kind of person in a specific situation.

This sounds easy. Listen to what happens when Jake asks real founders:

"Who exactly is your target customer?"

"Who exactly is your target customer?" Three co-founders, three different answers. And those were people building the same company.

🎓
💼
👥

Example: Busy college students who open BeReal irregularly and miss daily notifications.

Tip: a clear target user keeps your design focused and stops you from piling on extra features nobody asked for.
