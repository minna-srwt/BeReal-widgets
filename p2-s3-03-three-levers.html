Three Levers Behind Product Growth

Every product grows or stalls because of three forces. Think of them as levers you can push or pull. Benchmarking shows you which lever is powering a product, and which one is stuck.

A few of those measures sound technical. Here's what they mean in plain words:

Day-7 retentionof everyone who downloaded the app on a given day, how many are still using it a week later.
DAU/MAU ratioof the people who use an app in a month, what share use it on a typical day. If 100 people use it this month and 50 use it on an average day, the ratio is 50%. That's a sign of a daily habit.
ARPUtotal revenue divided by the number of users.

When you benchmark an app against its competitors, you're really asking three questions:

  1. AcquisitionAre people discovering it fast enough?
  2. RetentionAre they coming back often enough?
  3. MonetizationIs that loyalty turning into value?

The answers sit inside the metrics, but the insight comes from the comparison. If your acquisition is strong but your Day-7 retention is half your competitor's, you know where to focus next.
