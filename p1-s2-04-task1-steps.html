Step 2 of 3

Fill in Part A before you open BeReal

Answer the two questions in Part A:

1. Based on everything you've read so far, what do you expect BeReal to feel like?
2. What do you think will feel most different from the app you use most?

Keep each answer to 1–2 sentences. Don't look anything up. A guess is exactly what's being asked for: at the end of this step, you'll compare it with what actually happened.

✅You're on track if your answers mention something personal: your habits, the app you actually use, or a worry you actually have.
😵‍💫Stuck? Finish this sentence: "I bet the first thing that'll annoy or surprise me is…"

Step 3 of 3
Part 1

Open BeReal and Log Your Moments

Now open BeReal. Before tapping anything, write the date, time, and where you are at the top of the Session 1 table.

Then go through sign-up and look around the app as a new user would. Add a row every time something happens: a new screen, a question the app asks you, a choice you make, a wait, a surprise.

Part 2

Open BeReal and Log Your Moments

For each row, fill in:

What happenedThe screen or action, specific enough that someone else could find it
What I thoughtThe first sentence you'd have said out loud
How I feltA score from −2 (frustrated, lost) to +2 (delighted), with 0 for neutral
ScreenshotOptional, but useful later

Step 3 of 3
Part 3
📌Allow notifications when the app asks. The core BeReal moment arrives as a notification. Without it, there's nothing to log in the next task.
🤖No AI in this part. Your moment log is the one thing AI can't write for you, because it didn't open the app. Everything you build later in this step comes from this log.

Stop when you've finished sign-up and spent a few minutes on the home screen. If the app invites you to post right away, go ahead and log that too.

✅You're on track if you have at least 5 rows, and each one names a screen or an action, not just a feeling.
😵‍💫Only have 2 or 3 rows? You're probably logging too broadly. "Signed up" is really 3 or 4 moments: entering your number, the code, your name, the permissions. Split them up.
