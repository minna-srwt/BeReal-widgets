Search Engines (Google, Bing, Yahoo)

Your first stop? Search engines.

They're the front door to your market. They show you who's already visible to people looking for products like yours. Start by searching phrases a potential BeReal user might type:

"best social media apps for Gen Z"
"photo-sharing apps without filters"
"authentic social networking apps"

Industry Reports

Once you know who's visible, dig deeper into who's growing and who's dominating.

Sites like Statista and IBISWorld give a broader picture of market trends. Search for reports like "Social Media Market Share [current year]" or "Top Photo-Sharing Platforms."
