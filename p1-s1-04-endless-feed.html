You're all caught up

Not long ago, that used to be the finish line. For many years, from MySpace to early Facebook, feeds ended, refreshing content was deliberate, and you had to choose to see more.

Feeds weren't endless; they required an action, like "Next" or "Refresh," giving you space to pause.

As social media evolved into a mobile-first world, the rules changed.

algorithm

Instead of letting you dictate when to see more, apps handed over control to algorithms.

The feed became a never-ending river of posts, videos, stories — all demanding your attention.

Platforms like TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels removed the "next post" barrier and made video browsing passive and continuous.

That shift wasn't subtle. It was seismic.

The transition from curated, deliberate content to infinite scroll rewired how we engage — because engagement, not your wellbeing, was the metric being optimized.
