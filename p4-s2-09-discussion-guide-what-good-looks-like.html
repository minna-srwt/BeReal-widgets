What Good Looks Like

📌 These examples are a Discussion Guide for Spotify: Friday Fresh, continuing the Step 1 plan example. The details and numbers are made up for illustration. Notice what the strong one does: it asks what people noticed and what they'd do instead.

📏 The rubric

Questions that could prove you wrong · The test question

Could any answer make your "isn't working if" line come true?

0 · Not yetNo goal or "isn't working" line pasted, and no question connects to it
1 · Getting thereGoal and line pasted, but most questions are "nice to know", or none could make the line come true
2 · StrongEvery core question serves the goal, and at least one could make the "isn't working" line come true
Open, neutral, plain · The test question

Would a participant answer with a story, not a yes, a no or a compliment?

0 · Not yetMostly yes/no, leading or jargon questions
1 · Getting thereMostly open, but one or two nudge, use jargon, or stack two ideas
2 · StrongEvery question is open-ended, neutral, plain English, one idea at a time
A script you can run · The test question

Could you lead the session from this page alone?

0 · Not yetFewer than 5 or more than 7 core questions, or no follow-ups
1 · Getting there5–7 core questions, but the "instead" question, the "Why does that matter to you?" follow-up, the intro/wrap-up or the notes are missing
2 · Strong5–7 core questions across all three themes, 1–2 follow-ups each, the "instead" question and the "Why does that matter to you?" follow-up, intro and wrap-up questions, and a notes section

How it scores
Questions that could prove you wrong: 2 Every question serves the goal, and three could make the "isn't working" line come true: walking through the Friday mix shows whether they notice the change, and the ⭐ and "instead" questions show whether they'd keep playing.
Open, neutral, plain: 2 Every question invites a story ("Walk me through…", "How would this change…"), nothing nudges, and there's no jargon.
How it scores
A script you can run: 2 Seven core questions (2 · 2 · 3) in 37 minutes, a follow-up on each, the "instead" question, "Why does that matter to you?" after the ⭐ question, warm-up, wrap-up and notes.

Why it works: it asks what people noticed and what they'd do instead.
