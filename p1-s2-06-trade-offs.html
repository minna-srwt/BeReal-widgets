Every screen you saw was a decision someone made on purpose.

A permission request, a timer, a home screen with almost nothing on it.

And every decision trades one thing for another. That's what PMs call a trade-off: you can't get something without giving something else up.

So underneath every teardown is one question:

What did the team choose, and what did it give up to get it?
