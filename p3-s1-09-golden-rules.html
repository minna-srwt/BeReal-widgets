Great product designers don't wait for inspiration to strike.

They build creative systems, and AI can be one of them.

Used well, ChatGPT (or Claude) works like a brainstorming partner: it can generate many possible ideas, remix what competitors do, and explore edge cases a team might overlook.

But the secret is not to ask,"Give me an idea."

It's to ask better questions: prompts that include context, values, and goals.

If everyone outsources their thinking to AI, everyone ends up thinking about the same thing.

That's not how you learn to think, or how you build a point of view of your own. The AI gives you quantity. You bring the context, and you do the filtering.

The 3 golden rules for AI ideation
STAGE 1

Brief it

Who it's for · BeReal's values · Goal: more MAUs.
STAGE 2

Generate broadly

STAGE 3

Filter sharply

Only ideas that pass your MAU hypothesis.
Feed it context before creativity
Always start by telling the AI what it's aiming for:"You are brainstorming new social app features.""The app must stay true to BeReal's authenticity and low-pressure values.""Goal: increase monthly active users (MAUs) without creating addictive loops."This gives the AI direction, like briefing a product teammate.
Generate broadly, then filter sharply
Ask AI for 10–15 ideas, not 2 or 3. Then you become the filter:
  • Which ones actually make sense for BeReal?
  • Which feel off-brand, or impossible to build?
Always tie back to a hypothesis
For each idea, add a quick MAU hypothesis:"If we introduce this, more users will open the app because…"
