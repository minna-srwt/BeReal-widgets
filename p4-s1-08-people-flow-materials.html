Aim for a range of perspectives, not size.

  • Include both frequent BeReal users and occasional or new users.
  • Try to balance personalities and comfort levels with social media.
  • Avoid your very closest friends, who may hold back honest feedback.

If you can't gather everyone in person, a Zoom or Google Meet session works just as well.

Step 3: Plan your session flow

A clear structure keeps everyone focused and makes feedback easier to interpret later. Here's the recommended 30–45 minute flow:

1. Introduction · 5 minsWelcome participants, explain the purpose
Example Activities"We're testing a new BeReal feature. There are no right or wrong answers."
2. Use the BeReal App · 5 minsGround everyone in their current experience
Example ActivitiesAsk: "What do you like or dislike about BeReal today?"
3. Prototype Demo · 10 minsShow your wireframe
Example ActivitiesLet people click through without much explanation
4. Discussion · 15 minsCapture emotional and usability feedback
Example ActivitiesAsk open-ended questions (from your discussion guide)
5. Quick Survey · 5 minsGather rating-scale feedback
Example ActivitiesShare your Google Form / Typeform link

Total: 30–45 minutes. Keep each stage short and well-paced.

(A rating scale, sometimes called a Likert scale, just means questions like "How much do you agree, from 1 to 5?")

Step 4: Prepare your materials

Before your session:

✅Prototype ready: your wireframe link or file opened and clicked through once.
✅Discussion guide: 5–7 open-ended questions, printed or on screen.
✅Survey link: Google Form or Typeform ready to share.
✅Notes setup: a notebook, a doc, or a recording tool (with permission).

Optional: have a partner act as co-facilitator or note-taker so you can focus on the conversation.
