Downloads, MAU, revenue. But totals alone don't explain why growth is happening, or why it isn't.

Benchmarking metrics are comparative:

  • They show how well you perform relative to rivals.
  • They reveal meaningful gaps: what you're good at, where you lag, and where the opportunity is.
  • They become your lens for judging how well features turn into growth.

As the Qubstudio team puts it:

"Benchmarking reveals not just performance, but potential. It helps companies find what to do next to grow smarter, not just faster."

— Qubstudio, Product Growth Metrics & Benchmarks

So when you benchmark BeReal against its rival, you're not just comparing who has more users. You're looking for patterns that explain how those users behave: when they get started, what keeps them coming back, and when they start to drift away.
