📌 The competitor nobody puts on the list

Remember Help Scout? April Dunford pointed out that it often wasn't replacing Zendesk at all. It was replacing just email.

Help Scout
Zendesk
just email

April calls everything a product has to beat its competitive alternatives. The most overlooked one is the status quo: whatever people are doing right now instead of using any product at all, even if it's clunky.

competitive alternatives
Zendesk
Other help-desk tools
✉️ the status quo: just email

Here's why it matters. In April's world of business software, she says companies lose about 40% of their deals not to a rival, but to "no decision." In her words, they "lost to the spreadsheet... lost to pen and paper."

0%of deals lost to "no decision"

In other words, the biggest competitor was nothing new at all.

Taco Bell: direct
Burger truck: indirect
Vending machine: substitute
Leftovers from home: the status quo

Often the toughest one to beat.

Back at the taco truck, that's the office worker who just eats yesterday's leftovers at their desk. No truck, no vending machine. Just the easy, familiar option.

April's 40% number comes from business software, not social apps. But the question carries over perfectly:

When someone doesn't open BeReal, what are they doing instead?

Texting the group chat?Sending a Snapchat?Not sharing anything at all?

Keep that question in mind. You'll need it soon.
