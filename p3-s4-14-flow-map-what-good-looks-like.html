What Good Looks Like

📌 These examples are a flow map for Spotify: Friday Fresh, the feature from the Step 2 and Step 3 examples. The screens and copy are made up for illustration. Notice what the strong one does: it follows one listener through one Friday.

📏 The rubric

A flow anyone could follow · The test question

Could someone follow it from Screen 1 to "done" without asking a question?

0 · Not yetFewer than 4 screens, or more than 6, or no transitions
1 · Getting there4–6 screens, but one does two jobs, or a transition is missing or unclear
2 · Strong4–6 screens with one purpose each, a happy path to "done", and every transition labeled (action → what happens next)
States that feel alive · The test question

Does the flow show what happens when things aren't perfect?

0 · Not yetNo variations, or states missing
1 · Getting thereTwo variations and states listed, but a trigger is missing or the error is a dead end
2 · StrongTwo variations, all four states with triggers, and an error state that offers a way to recover
The key moment and the voice · The test question

Does the star test your hypothesis, and does the copy sound human?

0 · Not yetNo star, or no microcopy
1 · Getting thereA star, but it doesn't test the hypothesis, or microcopy is robotic ("Loading…", "Error code 400")
2 · StrongThe key moment tests the founding hypothesis, and microcopy on 3+ screens sounds like a friend, not a system

✅ Strong example

2 · 2 · 2
Flow Map (screens 1–2)
Intro banner"Friday Fresh is here: 3 new songs in your mix every Friday." · Tap "Try it" → opens Friday's Daily Mix
⭐ Friday Daily Mix"New this Friday" labels on 3 songs · Play a new song → reaction prompt; skip → next song
Flow Map (screens 3–5)
Reaction prompt"Keep songs like this coming?" · Tap 👍 or 👎 → saves the choice, back to the mix
Next Friday's nudge"Your Friday Fresh is ready." · Tap → opens the new mix (repeat user skips the intro)
Settings"Friday Fresh" toggle · Turn it off → mix goes back to normal
Two Variations
First-time vs. repeatFirst-timers see the intro banner; repeat users go straight to the Friday mix from the notification.
Success vs. errorIf the refresh fails, the mix loads without new songs and shows the error with "Try again".
States & Microcopy
Empty · Monday to Thursday"No new songs yet. Check back Friday."
Loading · Friday, while songs are picked"Finding 3 songs you'll like…"
States & Microcopy
Success · New songs added"Nice, 3 fresh songs in your mix."
Error · Refresh fails"Couldn't refresh your mix. Try again?"
Before You Map
Founding hypothesis…we believe they'll choose it over putting on a YouTube video in the background because it refreshes their own mix without any searching.
⭐ Key momentThe Friday mix screen, where the listener either plays the new songs or skips them.
How it scores
A flow anyone could follow: 2 Five screens, one job each, and every transition says what happens next, including the skip.
States that feel alive: 2 Both variations, all four states with triggers, and the error offers "Try again".
How it scores
The key moment and the voice: 2 The star is on the play-or-skip moment, exactly what the hypothesis bets on, and the copy sounds like Spotify, not a system.

Why it works: it follows one listener through one Friday, including when it goes wrong.
