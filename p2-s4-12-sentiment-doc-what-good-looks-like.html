What Good Looks Like

📌 These examples use Spotify: Daily Mix and Apple Music: Listen Now. The quotes and numbers are made up for illustration, and show 3 of the 5 themes. Notice what the strong one has: the person checking and questioning the AI at every Part.

📏 The rubric

Part A: Quotes you can trust · The test question

Are the quotes real, and is every theme about this one feature?

0 · Not yetNo quote check, or themes that fit any app ("people like it")
1 · Getting thereQuote check done, but a theme is broad, or all five themes are positive
2 · Strong5 sources opened and the results written down. Every theme could only be about this feature, and at least one is a complaint
Part B: A real comparison · The test question

Does the competitor table meet the same standard, and compare honestly with BeReal?

0 · Not yetQuotes unchecked, or the "Same or different?" column left empty or all "No match"
1 · Getting thereStandard met, but the comparison is thin (no "Same feeling, different cause")
2 · StrongQuotes checked, themes about this feature, and at least one "Same feeling, different cause" explained
Part C: Your verdict · The test question

Do both apps line up under shared categories, and does each insight say better how and for whom?

0 · Not yetCategories differ by app, or the verdict is "a tie" with no reason
1 · Getting thereShared categories, but insights miss for whom (or how), or no AI score is questioned
2 · Strong5–7 shared categories, matching scores, 3–5 insights that each say better how and for whom, and one AI score you disagree with
