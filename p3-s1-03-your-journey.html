By the end of this project, your BeReal feature will go from an idea in your head to something someone can actually tap through.

You'll create:

Act 1: Go Broad
Act 2: Lock It In
Act 3: Make It Real
1A shortlist of feature ideas scored with RICE (a simple way to rank ideas by how many people they reach, how much they matter, how sure you are, and how much work they take), plus the one idea you're backing.
2A PRD-lite, a short write-up of your feature covering the user problem, who it's for, how it would grow BeReal's monthly active users (MAUs), and what you're assuming is true.
3A flow map showing the key screens someone moves through when they use your feature.
4A clickable HTML wireframe, built with Claude or ChatGPT.

If "wireframe" is a new word, here's the difference between what you'll build and a finished app design:

Wireframe vs. finished design.

Wireframe (what you'll build)

  • Plain boxes and labels
  • Clickable, so you can move between screens
  • Shows how it works.

Finished design (not needed yet)

  • Colors, photos, branding
  • Shows how it looks.

A wireframe is like the blueprint of a house. It shows where the rooms are and how you walk between them, not what color the walls are.

That's all you need for people to understand your idea.
