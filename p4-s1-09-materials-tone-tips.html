Step 5: Keep the tone natural

You're facilitating a conversation, not running an interrogation.

🪑click

Use casual, curious language:

"What do you think?""How does this make you feel?""Would you change anything here?"

Avoid nudging people with phrases like "Do you like this cool feature?" Keep it neutral.

Pro Tips for Running a Smooth Session
🔦✅ Set expectations early: "We're testing the feature, not you."
✋✅ Don't explain screens. Let participants make sense of them first.
🤨✅ Watch body language: confusion often shows before it's spoken.
👋✅ End on a positive note: thank participants for their time and honesty.
