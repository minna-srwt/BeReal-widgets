What Good Looks Like

📌 These examples are a Build Doc for Spotify: Friday Fresh, continuing the Step 2–4 examples. The prompt, checks and friend test are made up for illustration. Notice what the strong one does: the AI did the building, the extern did the thinking.

📏 The rubric

It works, end to end · The test question

Can someone click from the first screen to "done" and back without getting stuck?

0 · Not yetNot clickable, or fewer than 4 screens, or no states
1 · Getting thereClickable, but one path is broken (often error or opt-out), or a state is missing
2 · Strong4–6 screens + 2–3 states, every button goes where the flow map says (error and opt-out too), and a clear beginning and end
Yours, not generic · The test question

Did you outsource the prototyping without outsourcing the thinking?

0 · Not yetA one-line prompt, no any-app test, or AI filler left in
1 · Getting therePrompt built from the blueprint, but the any-app test has no change, or some copy isn't yours
2 · StrongA prompt with every screen and destination, an any-app test with a named change, and every line of copy your own
A case for coming back · The test question

Would people come back, and can you explain why?

0 · Not yetNo hooks, or hooks that pressure ("lose your streak!"), or no paragraph
1 · Getting there2–3 gentle hooks, but the paragraph doesn't explain them, or there's no friend test
2 · Strong2–3 hooks that invite, a 5–7 sentence paragraph that explains how they grow MAUs, and something the friend actually did

How it scores

Why it works: built from the extern's own blueprint, and tested on a real person.
