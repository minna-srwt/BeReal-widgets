AI tools are your research assistants: quick, adaptable, and good at pulling scattered information together.

Using ChatGPT

You can use ChatGPT to get a quick competitive overview. Example prompt:

"List competitors for BeReal, including direct and indirect ones. Describe their main features and what makes each unique. Also: what do people do instead of using any of these apps to share moments with friends?"
Using Perplexity AI

Perplexity is useful when you want sources you can check. It cites where each fact comes from, so you can click through and confirm it. Example prompt:

"Who are BeReal's main competitors in the social media market? Include audience data, engagement rates, and global reach with cited sources."

It pulls from news articles, app rankings, and industry reports, giving you a fast snapshot with links.

Always open a couple of those links. A citation is only as good as the page it points to.

Notice what none of these tools will hand you directly: the status quo. Search engines and app charts list products. To find what people do instead of using a product at all, you have to ask the question yourself, which is exactly why the ChatGPT prompt above includes it.
