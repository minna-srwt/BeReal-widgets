Pro Tip

Your PRD-lite should read like a clear one-page brief.

If someone new joined your team tomorrow, they should instantly "get it": what you're making, why, and how success will be measured.

But keep in mind what a PRD can't do. Remember the team in Stockholm at the start of this project? They'd been stuck for years, and Knapp says they'd tried "to make the ideal PRD" along with the perfect pitch and the perfect design.

None of it got Google's leaders to back the idea.

A PRD gets your team on the same page. On its own, it rarely convinces anyone else.
