Great PMs bring together three ways of thinking.

Great PMs bring together three ways of thinking:

Lens Human Focus Understand people's needs, motivations, and frustrations.
Example

Why do users ignore daily BeReal notifications?

Lens Analytical Focus Use data to guide choices.
Example

How does daily posting rate affect retention?

Lens Strategic Focus Balance impact, effort, and alignment with company goals.
Example

Should we improve notifications or add a new feature?

To succeed, PMs also rely on soft skills:

💬clear communication
📖storytelling
🤝teamwork
🔄adaptability
