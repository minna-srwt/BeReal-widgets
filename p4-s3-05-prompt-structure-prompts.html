123Prompt structure for drafting surveys

When prompting AI, always include four elements:

Context · Explains what you're testing
"I'm running a focus group for a BeReal prototype."
Audience · Sets tone and difficulty
"Participants are college students aged 18–25."
Goal · Defines what to measure
"I want to understand ease of use, authenticity, and daily engagement potential."
Constraints · Keeps output short
"Limit to 7 questions, using a mix of 1–5 scales, yes/no, and open-ended."
123Example AI prompts (ready to copy)

💡 Prompt 1 — Brainstorm questions

I'm testing a new BeReal feature prototype that pairs two friends to post together once a day. Please generate 10 short, neutral survey questions that measure ease of understanding, usability, authenticity, and likelihood to use. Use a mix of 1–5 scale and yes/no questions.

AI might respond with:

1. How easy was it to understand what this feature does?2. How visually clear were the screens?3. How authentic does this feature feel for BeReal?4. Would you use this feature daily if it existed?5. Did the feature feel simple or complicated to use?6. How likely are you to recommend it to a friend?7. Would this make you open BeReal more often?8. Do you feel this fits naturally into the BeReal experience?9. What part of the feature did you like most?10. What would you change or improve?

🧠 Prompt 2 — Refine for quality

Take this list of questions and make them shorter, more conversational, and clearly worded for a Google Form. Keep them neutral and beginner-friendly.

This removes repeats and simplifies the wording, which is ideal for real participants.

✏️ Prompt 3 — Rephrase to match BeReal's tone

Rewrite these survey questions to sound natural and authentic, similar to BeReal's communication style (casual, simple, real). Avoid corporate terms like "user satisfaction" or "engagement."

AI output might look like: "How easy was it to get what this feature does?" · "Does this feel like something you'd want in BeReal?" · "Would this make using BeReal more fun or more stressful?" · "Would you try this again tomorrow?"

🧩 Prompt 4 — Add emotional or behavioral depth

Add 3 open-ended questions that explore emotion and behavior around using this BeReal feature. Keep them short.

AI could generate: "What did this feature make you feel?" · "When in your day would you actually use it?" · "If you could tweak one thing, what would it be?"

Use these in your survey's closing section to collect richer answers.

🧠Starting point, not the end-point. If everyone outsources their thinking to AI, everyone ends up thinking about the same thing. Use it to test your own thinking.
