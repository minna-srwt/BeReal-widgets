You don't need to know it. You'll describe your screens in plain English, and the AI writes the HTML for you. The result is a single file that opens in any web browser, where every button actually works.

That's powerful: what used to take a designer days now takes minutes. But John Zeratsky has seen what happens when teams lean on it too early:

"The more AI-generated or assisted they are, the more generic they tend to turn out."

Why? AI tools learned from the apps that already exist, so if you just ask for "a social app feature," you get something that looks like every other social app.

Teams in Jake and John's program who jumped straight to AI prototypes found theirs were "super generic. It didn't really describe what the product was."
