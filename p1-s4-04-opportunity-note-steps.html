A note on method

Gia's actual process for SparkToro started with surveying real customers, several of them, not one.

Your Data Organizer got you closer to that than your teardown alone: real numbers and real users' words. But you still haven't asked BeReal users anything directly, and app reviews lean toward people with strong feelings.

That's a real gap, not a technicality. Write your note with full confidence in what you found, and let your confidence label say how far it goes.

Step 2 of 6
Part 1

Define the Problem

Start from the broken moment in Box 4 of your Data Organizer. Then write it using this structure, from ProdPad:

[User type] experiences [problem] when [context], which results in [negative outcome or frustration].

ExampleInstagram users feel anxious about posting because they worry about how their content will perform immediately after posting, which leads to delays or fewer original posts.

Keep it short: one or two sentences that focus on the user's experience, not the app's feature.

Step 3 of 6
Part 2

Add the Evidence

Back up your problem with 2–3 pieces of evidence. Each one gets a row, and each row says where it came from:

a teardown moment # (what you saw yourself)
a Data Organizer box and row (a number from Box 1, or a quote # from Box 2)
ExampleTeardown, moment #4: hesitated right after receiving the daily notification.

Then copy your theme's label from Box 3: 🟢 pattern, 🟡 signal, or 🔴 conflict. Don't upgrade it. A 🟡 stays a 🟡 here.

Step 4 of 6
Part 3

Frame the Opportunity

Now state what could change if the problem were solved:

If we [describe the improvement or shift], [user type] may feel [___] and be more likely to [___].

ExampleIntroducing a more relaxed posting mode (like a "no likes for 24 hours" option) could help users share more authentically without fear of judgment.

Name one change, not a list of features. You're pointing at a direction, not designing the solution.

✅You're on track if your opportunity fixes the exact problem in Part 1, not a different one.

Step 5 of 6
Part 4

Describe the Potential Impact

Wrap it up by connecting to a measurable outcome:

What I'd measure: the one number that would tell you it worked.
How it connects to MAU: why that number would show up in monthly active users.
My confidence: Low, Medium, or High, and why.
ExampleReducing post anxiety could increase daily shares and story uploads, leading to higher user-generated content and overall engagement.
