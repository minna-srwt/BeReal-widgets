What makes a strong feature idea?

Before you start, internalize BeReal's three-word standard:

Simple.Human.Intentional.

Every idea you generate should pass this test:

Authenticit feels real, unfiltered, and spontaneous.
Focusedit works within BeReal's short-session model (post, connect, put the phone down).
Distinctit feels like something only BeReal could do.

Part B2

Keep only the ideas that pass:

🚫Violates authenticity or privacy? → Drop it.
⚙️Needs complex backend or moderation? → Drop it.
✅Feels light, human, and habit-forming? → Keep it.

Polish your top 5–7 ideas

Refine their wording so each is clear and testable.

Like Latchet's founders, make sure at least two of your ideas solve the same problem in different ways.

That gives you a real choice to make in the next step, not just a ranking of unrelated ideas.
