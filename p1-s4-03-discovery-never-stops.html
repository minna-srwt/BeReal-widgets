Gia often hears an objection from founders: they built the product to solve their own problem, so they already know the customer. Here's her answer:

"Products change, markets change, customers change, teams change… not everything can live inside of your head."

— Georgiana Laudi
productsmarketscustomersteams

Three reasons discovery never stops:

1

Users change. The people who loved BeReal at launch aren't the same people opening it today.

2

The product changes. Every new feature creates new moments that can work or break.

3

Every answer raises a new question. Your Data Organizer probably ended with at least one 🟡 signal or 🔴 conflict. That's not a failure. That's your next discovery question.

📌

Doesn't constant research slow everything down?

Gia says it doesn't have to. A survey can take "two or three weeks," and you can still "come to something decisive."

Discovery isn't about waiting until you're certain. It's about making the best call you can with what you know, then checking again.
