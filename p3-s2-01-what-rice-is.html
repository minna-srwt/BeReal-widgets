What RICE stands for

Tap each factor

What It MeansHow many users will this affect, and how often?
In Plain EnglishHow many people will use or benefit from this feature?
What It MeansHow much does it move your key metric (MAU)?
In Plain EnglishHow strongly it changes behavior: a small, medium, or big difference.
What It MeansHow sure are you about your estimates?
In Plain EnglishHow much real evidence (data or logic) supports your belief?
What It MeansHow long or hard is it to build and test?
In Plain EnglishHow much time, skill, or complexity is required?
