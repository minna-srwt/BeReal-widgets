Box 1

Your job: find 1–2 more numbers, and make sure at least one didn't come from BeReal itself.

Where BeReal's numbers actually live

BeReal is a private company, so you won't find quarterly reports like Duolingo's. And measurement firms (like Sensor Tower or Similarweb) usually keep their numbers behind paid accounts.

So almost every number about BeReal reaches the public through a news article. When an article quotes a measurement firm:

record the firm as who measured it (that's your "Source")
use the article as your link
🤖AI research tools can help you find sources. Never copy a number from an AI's answer. Click through and copy it from the original.
